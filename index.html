Hydro Clash - Privacy Policy

Developer: MUYUS LLC - Last updated: November 20, 2026

Information we collect

The Game does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

Data stored on your device

Settings (volume, language, selected country, arena) and basic game stats are stored only on your device.

In-app purchases

In-app purchases, if any, are processed entirely by Google Play; we do not receive payment details.

Third-party services

Google Play services may collect technical/crash data under Google's privacy policy.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

You can delete all game data by clearing app data or uninstalling.
